# Smart Media Transfer AI - Design System

## 1. Core Philosophy
The design system aims to provide a premium, modern, and unified ecosystem experience across both Windows (Smart Media Transfer AI Desktop) and Android (Smart Media Transfer AI).
It is a harmonious blend of Material 3 Expressive, Fluent Design (Windows 11), HyperOS, One UI, and Apple Human Interface.
The interface must evoke: Intelligence, Speed, Storage, Cloud, Synchronization, Technology, Trust, and Simplicity.

## 2. Color Palette
- **Primary:** Electric Blue `#2563EB`
- **Secondary:** Deep Purple `#7C3AED`
- **Accent:** Turquoise `#06B6D4`
- **Success:** Green `#22C55E`
- **Warning:** Orange `#F59E0B`
- **Error:** Red `#EF4444`

### Theme Modes
- **Light Mode Background:** Off-White `#FAFAFA`
- **Dark Mode Background:** Deep Black with Grey Nuances (e.g., `#0F111A`, surface `#1E1E24`)
- **Dynamic Color:** Slightly shifts based on the wallpaper or underlying layers.

## 3. Typography
- **Primary Fonts:** Google Sans, Roboto Flex, Inter.
- **Hierarchy:** Clear distinction between Headings, Subtitles, and Body.
- perfectly legible text with appropriate line spacing and tracking.

## 4. Visual Effects & Styles
- **Glassmorphism / Mica:** Used for backgrounds to give a subtle translucent feel.
- **Neumorphism:** Subtle inner and drop shadows to indicate depth without harsh borders.
- **Corner Radii:** Highly rounded corners for buttons and cards (e.g., 16px to 24px).
- **Glow & Highlights:** Subtle luminous effects on active elements and buttons.

## 5. Iconography
- **Style:** Material Symbols, Fluent Icons, Phosphor Icons.
- **Behavior:** Animated upon tap or hover.

## 6. Animations (60/120 FPS Target)
- **General:** Fluid page transitions, fade, zoom, slide, bounce.
- **Micro-interactions:** Button press sink-in, icon morphing.
- **Dashboard:** Circular progress bars animating on load, animated charts (bars, rings, curves).
- **Scanning:** Luminous wave passing over elements, categories filling up, real-time counters.
- **Transfer:** Immersive 2.5D isometric representation of Phone to PC, glowing particles representing files, speed-influenced animation speed, explosion/confetti on success.

## 7. UX Guidelines
- **One-handed usage:** Optimised layout for bottom-heavy interaction on Android.
- **Responsiveness:** Response time < 100ms.
- **Empty States:** Never show a blank screen. Always display an animation or useful information.

## 8. Shared Components
- **Buttons:** Deep press effects, haptic feedback (Android), glowing borders.
- **Cards:** Large, modern, rounded, depth shadows, animate on appearance.
